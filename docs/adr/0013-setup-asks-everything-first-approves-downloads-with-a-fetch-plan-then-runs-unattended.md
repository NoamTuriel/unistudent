# Setup asks everything first, approves downloads with a Fetch plan, then runs unattended

Course setup asks all its questions in one block of about ten minutes: university, course, language, folder, reading app, where the material comes from, anything else the course gave, the exam date, three separate Recording questions (transcribe and summarise, frame analysis, keep the video files), and consent to the speech-to-text and ffmpeg installs. Then it installs everything that needs the app restarted, together, scans the course site without downloading, and shows the Fetch plan. After the student's explicit OK and one grouped unit question ("I don't know" is an answer), it shows a box saying how long to leave the computer on, and runs without asking anything: installs, documents and the Wiki first, recordings downloaded, transcribed and summarised in parallel, a final Wiki update. A failure is retried once, then skipped and reported. The student comes back to one short report.

Setup transcribes the recordings without asking per recording because the Fetch plan, with real sizes, totals and a time estimate, is the approval: a student who said yes to transcripts and then OK'd a list of recordings has said yes to each of them. Asking again per recording was the reason a course with recordings was never ready when the student came back. `course-recordings` keeps its per-recording question for recordings added later, where no Fetch plan was shown.

All restarts are batched at the end of the question block because a plugin is only picked up when the app starts: a restart before the first course question (the old step 1) sent the student away before anything was answered, and a second one for the video-analysis tool would have done it again. The University plugin is therefore recommended and recorded in step 1, and installed with the video-analysis tool (through the Recommended-tool path of ADR 0012) after the last question, with one "close and reopen the app, then run setup again" message.

The sync never asks during a download because a download of tens of gigabytes takes hours and the student has left: every question it used to ask mid-download (which recordings, video or audio, which unit) is answered before it starts, from the course's Settings or the Fetch plan. Stream links, which expire, are collected only after the OK and re-collected once.
