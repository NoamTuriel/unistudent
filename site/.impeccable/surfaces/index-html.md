## Scope

The UniStudent landing page (site/index.html), Persuade mode. Audience: university students, non-technical first. Job: believe answers come only from their own course (trust first), see what it does with that trust, and install it in three steps.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a course reader the student has already marked up: claims from the course are highlighted and cited in the margin, anything else is flagged. It refuses the AI-tool landing default (dark hero, gradient, feature-card grid, code block).

OWN-WORLD: Coated white reader paper (#fbfbf8), ink (#1d1d1f), pencil grey (#4f5664) for handwritten margin notes, three highlighters with fixed meaning: yellow (#fff15a) = from your course, green (#a6f06a) = do this (actions, install), pink (#ff9ccb) = not from your course (the ⚠️ note). Literata for everything printed, Kalam for the margin hand. Running heads and page numbers; no cards, no boxes beyond the reader's own figures and tables.

STORY: The visitor reads that a general AI answers from everywhere while the exam grades the course's way; sees a made-up answer whose every paragraph is cited, and opens the cited page; sees what it does with the course; reads an honest NotebookLM comparison; installs in three steps.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Running head with page-number nav. Headline set large in Literata bold across the text column, its second sentence highlighted yellow by a stroke drawn once on load. One-sentence subhead, green-highlight Install button plus a link to the cited example answer (adaptation: the user's "trust first" answer makes the proof the second action; the video already sits directly below). The 35-second video as Figure 1 under it, autoplaying muted with captions. Margin notes in pencil beside the headline and figure.

FORM: Annotated course reader, my top-ranked grounded candidate (1 of 7), chosen by the user as the pick card. seed:0383682c6eb924e6

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: Citations are real: the margin note "Unit 2, p. 14" on the example answer opens the cited course page with the sentence highlighted.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
