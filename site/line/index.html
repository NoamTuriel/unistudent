U The Semester Line: your course, from day one to exam day

Study from your course, not the internet.

UniStudent makes your AI answer from your course's own files, with the exact page or lecture minute. It builds study packs with summaries, illustrations and an exercise guide, and you can ask it anything about your course.

U0 Start

UniStudent in 35 seconds

U1 Inbox

Drop in any file. It goes to the right subject.

PDFs, slides, Word and Excel files, scans, photos of your notes, lecture recordings. Put them in your course's inbox folder: UniStudent reads each one, sorts it into its subject, and adds it to what your AI knows.

With a plugin for your university, new material comes in from the course site by itself.

Your files stay in folders on your computer, and recordings are transcribed there too.

U2 Cited answers

Every answer stays on your course's line.

A general AI answers from everywhere: methods your course doesn't teach, other notation, other courses. Your exam grades your course's way. UniStudent keeps the AI on your course's line, and tells you when it leaves it.

You ask: Why does the demand curve slope down?

As the price rises, buyers purchase less of the good.

Subject 2, p. 14 (open the page)
2.3 The law of demand. When the price of a good rises, and nothing else changes, buyers purchase less of it. Part of the drop comes from buyers switching to a substitute: the substitution effect.

Think of coffee: if a cup goes from 3 to 6 euros, you start making it at home. That's the substitution effect from the same page, in an everyday case.

U5 Practice

Practice that builds up to the real exam.

Easy questions first, then real past exams. Before the test, every past-exam question is grouped by the method that solves it, with a link to each solution. Short on time? Each subject ends with the must-do questions.

U6 Interchange

Why not just use NotebookLM?

NotebookLM is a good research notebook, and it also answers from the sources you give it. UniStudent is built for one job: studying your course for its exam.

  1. Free, with no paid tiers.

    No plan to upgrade to and no limits of its own. It runs on the AI app you already use, or its free plan. MIT license ↗

  2. Your course's illustrations, redrawn.

    Graphs, flowcharts, circuits and molecules come back as clean drawings in your study pack, not just described in words.

  3. It's your AI, with everything it can already do.

    UniStudent lives inside the AI app you use, so every other tool you've added there works with your course too. Studying engineering, with a 3D modelling plugin installed? Ask for a 3D model of the part in question 6.

  4. It fetches and sorts your material.

    With a plugin for your university, new material comes in from the course site by itself. Anything else goes in the inbox and lands in the right subject. No uploading sources one by one.

  5. Made for students, from the first minute.

    Study packs per subject, recordings with the lecturer's announcements, practice that builds up to past exams. Nothing to set up or prompt your way into.

Like NotebookLM's audio overviews? Change here any time: the two lines run side by side.

U7 Board here

Board in three steps.

About five minutes, once. No coding: each step is a copy and a paste, or a few clicks.

  1. Install uv, the one helper UniStudent needs

    Open Terminal: press ⌘ and Space, type Terminal, press Enter. Paste this line and press Enter:

    curl -LsSf https://astral.sh/uv/install.sh | sh && echo 'export PATH="$HOME/.local/bin:$PATH"' >> ~/.zshenv

    The second half makes sure your AI app can find uv. Restart the app afterwards.

    Open PowerShell: press the Windows key, type PowerShell, press Enter. Paste this line and press Enter:

    powershell -ExecutionPolicy ByPass -c "irm https://astral.sh/uv/install.ps1 | iex"

    Restart your AI app afterwards.

    Open a terminal, paste this line and press Enter:

    curl -LsSf https://astral.sh/uv/install.sh | sh

  2. Add UniStudent to your AI app

    1. In the Claude desktop app, open Customize, then Plugins.
    2. Next to Personal plugins, click +, then Add marketplace.
    3. Choose Add from a repository and paste: NoamTuriel/unistudent
    4. Find unistudent in the list and click Install.

    Button names can differ a little between app versions.

    Type these two lines in Claude Code:

    /plugin marketplace add NoamTuriel/unistudent
/plugin install unistudent@unistudent

    Add this to the app's MCP settings (Cursor, Claude Desktop chat, Windsurf, Gemini CLI's ~/.gemini/settings.json):

    {
  "mcpServers": {
    "unistudent": {
      "command": "uvx",
      "args": ["--from", "git+https://github.com/NoamTuriel/unistudent#subdirectory=plugins/unistudent", "unistudent-mcp"]
    }
  }
}

    VS Code calls the top key servers and wants "type": "stdio". On a Mac, if it doesn't start, put the full path from which uvx in "command".

    Paste this in your terminal:

    codex mcp add unistudent -- uvx --from "git+https://github.com/NoamTuriel/unistudent#subdirectory=plugins/unistudent" unistudent-mcp

  3. Set up your first course

    Open a new chat and say: Set up my course with UniStudent. In Claude Code you can also type /unistudent:course-setup.

    It asks one question at a time: your university, your course, where your files are. Then it reads your course and tells you what's next.

    Later, when you get new material, drop it in the course's inbox folder and say Add my new files.

    You can stop setup at any point; running it again picks up where you left off. Recordings also need ffmpeg, and setup offers to install it.
