A general AI answers from everywhere: methods your course doesn't teach, other notation, other courses. Your exam grades your course's way. UniStudent keeps the AI inside your course material, and tells you when it steps outside.

Why does the demand curve slope down?

As the price rises, buyers purchase less of the good.

Unit 2 · p. 14 2.3 The law of demand. When the price of a good rises, and nothing else changes, buyers purchase less of it. Part of the drop comes from buyers switching to a substitute: the substitution effect. (A made-up course page.)

Think of coffee: if a cup goes from 3 to 6 euros, you start making it at home. That's the substitution effect from the same page.Unit 2 · p. 14

⚠️ Not from your course material (general knowledge): behavioral economists also explain this through loss aversion.

A made-up example for an intro economics course. Click the first citation to open its page.

What it does

Your course, organized for studying.

You give it the course. It gives you back the course, ready to study, with every line traceable to where it came from.

A study pack for every unit

What to know by heart, what to understand, common mistakes, the kinds of exercises you'll meet. Your course's graphs and diagrams are redrawn too.

Know by heart

  • The law of demand, in the course's words
  • The notation: Qd, P, ε

Understand

  • Why a price change moves you along the curve
  • Common mistake: mixing the two up

Drop in any file

PDFs, slides, Word, Excel, scans, photos of notes, recordings. It reads each one and sorts it into the right unit.

Recordings you can search

A timed table of contents for every lecture. Announcements and "this will be on the exam" are written down and highlighted.

  1. 00:03:40The demand curve, defined
  2. 00:41:12Announcement: send me your project partner's name by next Tuesday.
  3. 01:02:30On the exam: a shift of the curve vs. a move along it.

Practice up to the real exam

Easy questions first, then real past exams. Before the test, every past-exam question is grouped by the method that solves it, with a link to each solution.

Your files stay yours

Everything lives in folders on your computer. Recordings are transcribed locally.

Any university, any course

Extra plugins add downloads from your university's site and rules for your subject.

Plain-language setup

One question at a time. No coding, and it tells you what to do next.

Compared

Why not just use NotebookLM?

NotebookLM is a good research notebook, and it also answers from the sources you give it. UniStudent is built for one job: studying your course for its exam.

01

Free, with no paid tiers

No plan to upgrade to and no limits of its own. It runs on the AI app you already pay for, or its free plan.

02

Illustrations, redrawn

Graphs, flowcharts, circuits and molecules from your course come back as clean drawings, not just described in words.

03

It's your AI, with all its tools

It lives inside the AI app you use, so every tool you've added there works with your course. Engineering student with a 3D plugin? Ask for a 3D model of the part in question 6.

04

It fetches and sorts your material

With a plugin for your university, new material comes in from the course site by itself. Anything else goes in the inbox folder and lands in the right unit. No uploading sources one by one.

05

Made for students, out of the box

Study packs per unit, recordings with the lecturer's announcements, practice that builds up to past exams. Nothing to set up or prompt your way into.

Like NotebookLM's audio overviews? Keep them. The two work fine side by side.

Install

Start in three steps

About five minutes, once. No coding: each step is a copy and a paste, or a few clicks.

  1. Install uv, the one helper UniStudent needs

    Open Terminal: press ⌘ and Space, type Terminal, press Enter. Paste this line and press Enter:

    curl -LsSf https://astral.sh/uv/install.sh | sh && echo 'export PATH="$HOME/.local/bin:$PATH"' >> ~/.zshenv

    The second half makes sure your AI app can find uv. Restart the app afterwards.

    Open PowerShell: press the Windows key, type PowerShell, press Enter. Paste this line and press Enter:

    powershell -ExecutionPolicy ByPass -c "irm https://astral.sh/uv/install.ps1 | iex"

    Restart your AI app afterwards.

    Open a terminal, paste this line and press Enter:

    curl -LsSf https://astral.sh/uv/install.sh | sh

  2. Add UniStudent to your AI app

    1. In the Claude desktop app, open Customize, then Plugins.
    2. Next to Personal plugins, click +, then Add marketplace.
    3. Choose Add from a repository and paste: NoamTuriel/unistudent
    4. Find unistudent in the list and click Install.

    Button names can differ a little between app versions.

    Type these two lines in Claude Code:

    /plugin marketplace add NoamTuriel/unistudent
/plugin install unistudent@unistudent

    Add this to the app's MCP settings (Cursor, Claude Desktop chat, Windsurf, Gemini CLI's ~/.gemini/settings.json):

    {
  "mcpServers": {
    "unistudent": {
      "command": "uvx",
      "args": ["--from", "git+https://github.com/NoamTuriel/unistudent#subdirectory=plugins/unistudent", "unistudent-mcp"]
    }
  }
}

    VS Code calls the top key servers and wants "type": "stdio". On a Mac, if it doesn't start, put the full path from which uvx in "command".

    Paste this in your terminal:

    codex mcp add unistudent -- uvx --from "git+https://github.com/NoamTuriel/unistudent#subdirectory=plugins/unistudent" unistudent-mcp

  3. Set up your first course

    Open a new chat and say:

    Set up my course with UniStudent.

    In Claude Code you can also type /unistudent:course-setup. It asks one question at a time: your university, your course, where your files are. Then it reads your course and tells you what's next.

    Later, when you get new material, drop it in the course's inbox folder and say Add my new files.

    You can stop setup at any point; running it again picks up where you left off. Recordings also need ffmpeg, and setup offers to install it.

Set it up once. Study from it all semester.
