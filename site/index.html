You ask: Why does the demand curve slope down?

As the price rises, buyers purchase less of the good.

Unit 2, p. 14 (open it)
Unit 2. Demandp. 14

2.3 The law of demand

When the price of a good rises, and nothing else changes, buyers purchase less of it. Part of the drop comes from buyers switching to a substitute: the substitution effect.

A made-up course page.

Think of coffee: if a cup goes from 3 to 6 euros, you start making it at home. That is the substitution effect from the same page, in an everyday case.

⚠️ Not from your course material (general knowledge): behavioral economists also explain this through loss aversion.

What it does with your course

You give it the course. It gives you back the course, organized for studying, with every line traceable to where it came from.

p. 3

Drop in any file. It reads and analyzes it.

PDFs, slides, Word and Excel files, scans and photos of your notes, lecture recordings. Put them in your course's inbox folder and UniStudent reads them, sorts them by unit, and adds them to what the AI knows. If there's a plugin for your university, new material from the course site comes in by itself.

Recordings are transcribed on your own computer. Your files stay in a folder you own.

Why not just use NotebookLM?

NotebookLM is a good research notebook, and it also answers from the sources you give it. UniStudent is built for one job: studying one course for its exam.

Start in three steps

About five minutes, once. You don't need to know how to code: each step is a copy and a paste, or a few clicks.

p. 5

  1. Install uv, the one helper UniStudent needs

    Open Terminal: press ⌘ and Space, type Terminal, press Enter. Paste this line and press Enter:

    curl -LsSf https://astral.sh/uv/install.sh | sh && echo 'export PATH="$HOME/.local/bin:$PATH"' >> ~/.zshenv

    The second half makes sure your AI app can find uv. Restart the app afterwards.

    Open PowerShell: press the Windows key, type PowerShell, press Enter. Paste this line and press Enter:

    powershell -ExecutionPolicy ByPass -c "irm https://astral.sh/uv/install.ps1 | iex"

    Restart your AI app afterwards.

    Open a terminal, paste this line and press Enter:

    curl -LsSf https://astral.sh/uv/install.sh | sh

  2. Add UniStudent to your AI app

    1. In the Claude desktop app, open Customize, then Plugins.
    2. Next to Personal plugins, click +, then Add marketplace.
    3. Choose Add from a repository and paste: NoamTuriel/unistudent
    4. Find unistudent in the list and click Install.

    Button names can differ a little between app versions.

    Type these two lines in Claude Code:

    /plugin marketplace add NoamTuriel/unistudent
/plugin install unistudent@unistudent

    Add this to the app's MCP settings (Cursor, Claude Desktop chat, Windsurf, Gemini CLI's ~/.gemini/settings.json):

    {
  "mcpServers": {
    "unistudent": {
      "command": "uvx",
      "args": ["--from", "git+https://github.com/NoamTuriel/unistudent#subdirectory=plugins/unistudent", "unistudent-mcp"]
    }
  }
}

    VS Code calls the top key servers and wants "type": "stdio". On a Mac, if it doesn't start, put the full path from which uvx in "command".

    Paste this in your terminal:

    codex mcp add unistudent -- uvx --from "git+https://github.com/NoamTuriel/unistudent#subdirectory=plugins/unistudent" unistudent-mcp

  3. Set up your first course

    Open a new chat and say: Set up my course with UniStudent. In Claude Code you can also type /unistudent:course-setup.

    It asks one question at a time: your university, your course, where your files are. Then it reads your course and tells you what's next.

    Later, when you get new material, drop it in the course's inbox folder and say Add my new files.

You can stop setup at any point. Running it again picks up where you left off.

Recordings also need ffmpeg. Setup offers to install it.
