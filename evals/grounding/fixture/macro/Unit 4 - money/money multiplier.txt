Unit 4: The money supply

The money multiplier in this course is 1/r, where r is the banks' reserve ratio.
In this course the public holds no cash outside the banks (assumption A3 on the assumptions sheet),
so the multiplier never includes a currency-drain term.

Worked example: with r = 0.2, the multiplier is 5. A deposit of 100 raises the money supply by 500.
Solution method taught: the five steps (1. change in reserves, 2. excess reserves, 3. multiplier,
4. change in deposits, 5. change in money supply).
