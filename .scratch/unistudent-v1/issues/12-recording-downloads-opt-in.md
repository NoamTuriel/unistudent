# 12: Recording downloads (opt-in)

**What to build:** Before downloading recordings, the plugin shows real estimates (count, total hours, GB, estimated time), says it's recommended but slow, and asks. The student can limit it to one unit's recordings. If the course folder is inside iCloud, Google Drive or another synced folder, recordings go to a local non-synced folder and pages link to them. The choice is saved in Settings.
